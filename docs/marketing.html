Plato

Notes that vanish. Like Snapchat, but for your thoughts.

Write it down. Set a timer. Watch it disappear. Simple as that.

Disappearing Notes

Every note has a countdown. Choose 5 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours, 3 days, or 7 days. When the timer hits zero, it's gone forever. No recovery. No traces.

Live Countdown

Watch the time tick down in real time. A ring drains beside each note and the text fades as it nears zero. You'll always know exactly how much time remains.

Burn on Demand

Can't wait for the timer? Swipe a note or tap Burn Now to destroy it instantly. Once burned, it can never be recovered.

Light, Clean and Native

Built like the apps that come with your iPhone, with Light, Dark and System appearances. The icon is Plato's wax tablet of memory, and a line from Plato greets you when every note is gone.

Completely Offline

No accounts. No cloud. No internet required. Zero network requests. Your notes exist on your device and nowhere else.

100% Open Source

Plato is free, open source, and licensed under MIT. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. The entire codebase is on GitHub for anyone to inspect, fork, and contribute to.
